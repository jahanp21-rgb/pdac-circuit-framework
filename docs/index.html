Central Hypothesis

Why does PDAC resist every drug?

Over fifty molecularly targeted agents have failed in PDAC clinical trials. This study proposes that the answer lies not in individual pathway failure, but in a structurally redundant, three-circuit survival architecture.

pH / Hypoxia Circuit

KRAS→HIF1A→CA9/CA12 axis enables tumour cells to acidify the extracellular environment and sustain glycolytic metabolism under hypoxia.

CA9 · CA12 · HIF1A · EGLN1 · HSP90AA1
Target drug: SLC-0111 (CA9/CA12 inhibitor)

Death Suppression Circuit

BCL2-family proteins physically sequester CASP9 and BAX, preventing apoptosome assembly despite oncogene-driven replication stress.

BCL2 · BCL2L1 · CASP9 · BAX · FADD · APAF1
Target drug: Venetoclax / BCL2 family inhibitors

Cell Cycle Circuit

CDK4-Cyclin D1 complex phosphorylates the retinoblastoma protein, driving unrestrained G1→S progression and sustained mitotic cycling.

CDK4 · CCND1 · TTK · SLC29A1
Target drug: Palbociclib (CDK4/6 inhibitor)
⟷
The coupling hypothesis: These three circuits are not independent — they share regulatory nodes including RELA (NF-κB p65), which bridges all three arms. Single-circuit targeting allows the remaining circuits to compensate. Only simultaneous three-circuit disruption may prevent adaptive rewiring.

Important Limitations and Caveats
Computational only. No wet laboratory experiments were performed. All findings require experimental validation in cell line, organoid, and in vivo models.
Classifier circularity. The internal AUC of 1.0 is expected — subtype labels were derived from the same circuit scores used as classifier inputs. External AUC of 0.742 is the meaningful metric.
Wide confidence intervals. HR 95% CIs (1.75–66.96 in discovery; 2.89–50.43 in validation) reflect exploratory cohort sizes. Effect magnitudes require prospective confirmation.
Boolean simplification. Binary gene logic cannot capture continuous biochemical dynamics. All Boolean predictions are qualitative and topology-dependent.
Subtype B small n. 11 patients in Subtype B. Survival findings for this subgroup are hypothesis-generating only.
pH arm in vitro. CA9 and HIF1A show minimal CRISPR dependency in normoxic cell culture. This does not negate their patient-tumour relevance.
