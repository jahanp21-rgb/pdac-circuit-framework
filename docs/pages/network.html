⚠ Gemcitabine is modelled as TP53 = 1 (Boolean ON), which in the model produces 100% death trajectories. This is a simplification — in reality, BCL2L1-mediated CASP9 sequestration limits actual apoptotic execution even when TP53 is activated. The Boolean model overestimates single-agent gemcitabine efficacy. All results should be interpreted as model-dependent and qualitative.

Critical insight: RELA and BCL2 are the only two single nodes whose individual inactivation produces complete circuit collapse (+49.6 percentage points each). This identifies them as priority single-node therapeutic targets at the convergence of all three circuit arms. By contrast, CDK4, CCND1, and TTK produce zero change when individually knocked out, providing a computational explanation for why CDK4/6 inhibitors have shown minimal single-agent activity in PDAC despite confirmed genetic essentiality.
