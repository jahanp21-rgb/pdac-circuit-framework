This pattern is consistent with — but does not prove — the known biochemical interaction in which BCL2 physically sequesters CASP9 through BH3-domain binding. CCND1 and BCL2L1 are positively correlated (r = 0.58), consistent with shared NF-κB-dependent transcriptional co-regulation of the cell cycle and death-suppression arms. ⚠ Correlation does not establish causation or mechanism. These data are consistent with coupled circuit architecture but cannot prove regulatory directionality or biochemical interaction in patient tumours. Pathologic stage was not independently significant in the same model (p = 0.096). Circuit-score model C-index = 0.647 vs clinical-only 0.589. ⚠ The 95% CI of 1.75–66.96 is very wide. This reflects the exploratory cohort size and means the true population hazard ratio is imprecisely estimated. These results should not be used for individualised risk communication without prospective validation in a larger cohort. [ Insert Figure 3: Cox forest plot, C-index comparison, decision curve analysis, calibration curves — generated by R/04_survival_analysis.R ] External Cohort Validation (GSE183795, n = 134) Key finding: Discovery-cohort Cox coefficients applied without retraining to 134 patients at a separate institution on Affymetrix microarray reproduced the primary finding: Cell Cycle CDS HR = 12.08 (95% CI 2.89–50.43, p = 0.0006). The two hazard ratios differ by only 0.15 units despite different platforms, institutions, and patient populations. This is the most methodologically rigorous finding in the study. [ Insert Figure 4: Cross-cohort forest plot and external ROC curves — generated by R/05_external_validation.R ] CRISPR Functional Dependency (DepMap, 47 PDAC Lines) Key finding: Four circuit genes exceed the DepMap essentiality threshold of −0.5: CCND1 (−1.13), BCL2L1 (−0.83), TTK (−0.82), CDK4 (−0.50). This provides functional, loss-of-viability evidence independent of patient expression data that these genes are causally required for PDAC cell survival. KRAS serves as internal positive control (−1.30). ⚠ CA9 and HIF1A show minimal CRISPR dependency scores due to normoxic cell culture conditions in which the HIF1A–CA9 axis is constitutively inactive. This does not negate their clinical relevance in patient tumours.
